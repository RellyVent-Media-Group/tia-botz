## 5. Asset Production Guidelines

### Brand Consistency
- **Color Palette:**
  - Mary: Electric Blue (#00BFFF), Cyan (#00FFFF), Purple (#800080)
  - Mac: Warm Brown (#8B4513), Forest Green (#228B22), Gold (#FFD700)
  - System: White (#FFFFFF), Dark Gray (#333333), Accent Red (#FF0000)

- **Typography:**
  - Headlines: Modern sans-serif (Inter, Roboto, or similar)
  - Body text: Clean, readable sans-serif
  - Character quotes: Distinct font for each character's voice

- **Logo Usage:**
  - TaK-Tiapmaatzu OS logo always prominent
  - Consistent sizing and placement
  - Clear space around logo

### Character Voice Visual Representation
- **Mary:** Sharp angles, digital effects, cool colors, tech elements
- **Mac:** Organic shapes, warm lighting, natural textures, human elements
- **Combined:** Blending of both styles where they interact

### File Naming Convention
- `platform_type_description_version.extension`
- Example: `instagram_quote_mary-privacy_v1.png`

### Quality Standards
- **Resolution:** Minimum 72 DPI for web, 300 DPI for print
- **Format:** PNG for web, SVG for scalable graphics
- **Accessibility:** Alt text for all images, high contrast ratios
