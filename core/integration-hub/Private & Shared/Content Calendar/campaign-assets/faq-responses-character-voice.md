## Security & Privacy Questions

### Q: How secure is my data really?

**Mary Magnumbytes:**  
Let me be precise: your data is encrypted using AES-256 before it ever touches storage. That's the same standard the NSA uses for classified information. We use zero-knowledge architecture, meaning we literally cannot access your data even if we wanted to—your encryption keys never leave your control. The system is designed so that data breaches are mathematically impossible, not just difficult.

**Mac Nazarene:**  
Security is more than encryption—it's about trust. We don't mine your data, sell it, or use it to train models that benefit other customers. Your data serves your purposes alone. ### Q: Will you use my data to train AI models?

**Mary Magnumbytes:**

**Mac Nazarene:**  
Your business data is your intellectual property, not our training material. We believe that AI should learn from your expertise to serve you better, not harvest your expertise to serve others. The system is designed to respect the ownership of ideas and patterns. What makes your business unique should stay unique to your business.

### Q: What happens if there's a security breach?

**Mary Magnumbytes:**  
In a traditional system, you'd be in trouble. In our system, the attackers would get encrypted gibberish even if they breached our servers. Remember zero-knowledge? We don't have the keys. But we also have intrusion detection that operates on mathematical anomalies—if someone tries to access data they shouldn't, the system detects it before they can even attempt decryption.

**Mac Nazarene:**  
Transparency is our security philosophy too. If there's ever an attempted breach, you'll know about it immediately—within minutes, not weeks. We don't hide security incidents or bury them in legal language. And because the system is designed with reversible operations, even if someone did gain access, the damage would be limited and quickly correctable.
