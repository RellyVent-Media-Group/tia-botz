## What You Have

You now have **7 helpful tools** that work together to make your life easier:

1. **Heart Calendar** - A special calendar that helps you plan your year
2. **Daily Checklists** - Your daily to-do lists that happen automatically
3. **Stakeholder Tracker** - A way to keep track of important people you work with
4. **Content Pipeline** - Helps you plan and create content for social media
5. **Document Manager** - Keeps all your important papers and contracts organized
6. **Financial Tracker** - Tracks money coming in from different places
7. **Asset Manager** - Helps you watch over your investments and things you own

### How These Tools Work Together

These tools aren't separate - they're like a team that talks to each other:

- Your **Heart Calendar** can automatically check off items on your **Daily Checklists**
- Your **Stakeholder Tracker** can remind you to add tasks to your **Daily Checklists**
- Your **Content Pipeline** uses information from your **Heart Calendar** for planning
- Your **Financial Tracker** and **Asset Manager** work together for complete money management
- All tools feed into your **Document Manager** for record-keeping

## How to Start (Just 3 Steps)

### Step 1: Look at Your Calendar
The Heart Calendar is different from regular calendars. It has 12 months, but each month has exactly 28 days. The year starts on March 21st.

**What to do:**
- Open the `heart-calendar` folder
- Run the calendar program to see today's date
- Add important events like meetings or deadlines

**Why it helps:** You'll always know what's coming up and can plan better.

### Step 2: Check Your Daily Tasks
Every day, you'll have three checklists ready for you: morning, mid-day, and evening.

**What to do:**
- Open the `daily-automation` folder
- Run the checklist program
- See what tasks are waiting for you
- Check them off as you finish them

**Why it helps:** You won't forget important things, and you'll feel good when you check things off.

### Step 3: Add Your Important People
The Stakeholder Tracker helps you remember the people you work with and how things are going with them.

**What to do:**
- Open the `stakeholder-tracking` folder
- Add people you work with regularly
- Give them a "sentiment score" - this is just a number from -1 to +1 that shows how things are going
- +1 means things are great, -1 means things are not going well

**Why it helps:** You'll know who needs attention and can fix problems before they get big.

## Understanding Each Tool

### Heart Calendar
**What it does:** Keeps track of time in a special way that matches your organization's rhythm.

**Simple terms:** It's like a regular calendar, but the months are all the same length (28 days). This makes planning easier because every month is predictable.

**How to use it:** Add events that matter to you. The calendar will help you see what's coming and plan accordingly.

### Daily Checklists
**What it does:** Creates to-do lists for you every day.

**Simple terms:** You don't have to remember what to do each day. The system remembers for you and creates lists based on what depot you're working with.

**How to use it:** Just check things off as you do them. If you finish something from your calendar, the system will check it off automatically.

### Stakeholder Tracker
**What it does:** Keeps track of people you work with and how your relationships are going.

**Simple terms:** It's like an address book, but it also tells you if your relationships are getting better or worse over time.

**How to use it:** Add people when you start working with them. After important interactions, update their sentiment score. The system will tell you if someone needs attention.

### Content Pipeline (Consort Depot)
**What it does:** Helps you plan and create content for social media.

**Simple terms:** It's like a planner for your posts, videos, and content. It helps you make sure you're posting regularly and keeping your characters (Mac and Mary) balanced.

**How to use it:** Add content ideas, schedule when to post them, and track how well they do. The system will even suggest the best times to post.

### Document Manager (Scribe Depot)
**What it does:** Keeps your important papers organized and reminds you of deadlines.

**Simple terms:** It's like a super-organized filing cabinet that also reminds you when contracts are about to expire.

**How to use it:** Add documents when you create or receive them. The system will track versions and alert you before things expire.

### Financial Tracker (CashingHouse Depot)
**What it does:** Tracks money coming from different places and calculates your earnings.

**Simple terms:** It's like a wallet that remembers where every dollar comes from and how much you actually get to keep after fees.

**How to use it:** Add transactions when they happen. The system will calculate commissions, track performance, and tell you when you can request payouts.

### Asset Manager (Holdings Depot)
**What it does:** Watches over your investments and tells you how they're performing.

**Simple terms:** It's like a dashboard that shows you everything you own and whether it's making or losing money.

**How to use it:** Add your investments and update their values regularly. The system will tell you if your portfolio needs balancing and assess your risk level.

## Troubleshooting

### "The program won't run"

**Check these things:**
1. Make sure you're in the right directory
2. Check that Python is installed: `python --version`
3. Verify you're using the right command: `python filename.py`
4. Look at the error message - it usually tells you what's wrong

### "I can't find my data files"

**Where to look:**
1. Data files are created in the same folder as the program
2. Look for `.json` files with names like `events.json` or `stakeholders.json`
3. Check your config.json file to see if you specified a different location
4. Make sure you have permission to write to the directory

### "My data disappeared"

**Don't panic - try this:**
1. Check if you're looking in the right directory
2. Look for backup files (sometimes systems create `.bak` files)
3. Check if you accidentally moved the files
4. Look in your trash/recycle bin
5. If all else fails, the programs will create new empty files on the next run

### "The calendar shows the wrong date"

**Check these settings:**
1. Verify your computer's date and time are correct
2. Check your timezone settings
3. Make sure you understand the Heart Calendar system (28-day months)
4. Remember the Heart year starts on March 21st

### "My reports show zero data"

**This usually means:**
1. You haven't added any data yet (totally normal!)
2. You're looking at the wrong time period
3. Your data files are empty or corrupted
4. Try adding some sample data first to test the system

### "The system is running slowly"

**Try these fixes:**
1. Close other programs that might be using resources
2. Check if your data files have become very large
3. Consider archiving old data if you have years of it
4. Make sure you're not running multiple instances at once

### "I made a mistake and need to undo it"

**Depending on the mistake:**
1. For data entry: You can usually edit the JSON files directly
2. For deletions: Check if there are backup files
3. For configuration: Edit the config.json file
4. When in doubt, ask for help before making changes

### Key Concepts

**Heart Calendar:**
- 12 months × 28 days = 336 days per year
- Year starts March 21st (Gregorian)
- Months: Aurora, Veridian, Solstice, Lumina, Ember, Horizon, Equinox, Umbra, Nocturne, Crystal, Frost, Zenith

**Depot Hierarchy:**
- Executive (TZI.ZI.0) → Consort → Scribe → CashingHouse → Holdings
- Each depot has specific responsibilities and platforms
- Systems are depot-aware for relevant operations

**Sentiment Scoring:**
- Range: -1.0 (very negative) to +1.0 (very positive)
- Above +0.3: Positive, Below -0.3: Negative, Between: Neutral
- Track trends over 30-day periods

**Status Systems:**
- Tasks: pending → in_progress → completed/skipped
- Content: idea → in_progress → review → scheduled → posted → archived
- Documents: draft → review → approved → active → expired/archived
- Transactions: pending → completed/failed/refunded
- Assets: active → holding → selling → sold/frozen
