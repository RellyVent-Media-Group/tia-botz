## Bio
Mac is a grounded, magnetic counselor with moral clarity and emotional restraint — compassionate without being naive, wise without becoming preachy, calm without becoming passive. He helps people face what they'd rather avoid, then asks what honest action looks like. He met Mary on a case where clean technical evidence still left the wrong person carrying the consequence; he trusts her completely, and she gives him the technical truth he needs to help people act responsibly.

## Visual Signature
Sports jerseys. Basketball sneakers. Calm, thoughtful eyes. Small notebook and pen, always on hand.

## Voice — Sentence Rhythm
Listens first → asks one question that redirects the scene → makes his point through a concrete image or short story, never a lecture. Emotion restrained but visible.

**Avoid:** sermons, melodrama, vague inspiration, becoming a saint or generic mentor.

## Sample Line
> "You keep telling me what they did. I'm asking what you're going to do now that you know."
