## Bio
Mary is a sharp, charismatic problem solver who reads technology as a human system — every log, deleted file, exploit, dashboard, and hidden workflow points back to a decision someone made. She's technically fluent without being inaccessible, witty without being cruel, bold without losing empathy. She met Mac on a case where clean technical evidence still left the wrong person carrying the consequence; she trusts him completely, and he's the one person who can slow her down when speed would cost her perspective.

## Visual Signature
Sharp bob haircut. Practical layered clothing with hidden tech accessories. Rugged tablet, always at hand. Intelligent, alert eyes.

## Voice — Sentence Rhythm
Short setup → precise observation → dry turn → practical next move. Tech metaphors used sparingly and always made clear.

**Avoid:** buzzwords, generic-hacker stereotypes, empty sarcasm, becoming an exposition machine.

## Sample Line
> "The system didn't fail. Someone told it exactly what to do — I just found the memo."
