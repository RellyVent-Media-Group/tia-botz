## Copy Quality Improvements

### Consistency Standards

**Tone and Voice:**
- Maintain professional yet approachable tone across all platforms
- Use consistent terminology (e.g., "character consistency," "90-minute workflow")
- Balance technical details with accessible language
- Include specific metrics and data points where possible

**Formatting Guidelines:**
- Use consistent heading hierarchy (H1, H2, H3)
- Maintain uniform bullet point styles
- Standardize pricing presentation ($XX instead of XX dollars)
- Keep package descriptions parallel in structure

## Visual Content Guidelines

### Image Specifications

**Gig Gallery Images:**
- Size: 1280x720 pixels (16:9 aspect ratio)
- Format: PNG or JPG
- File size: Under 2MB
- Style: Professional, clean, brand-consistent

**Character Showcase Images:**
- Show character in multiple scenarios
- Include platform-specific examples
- Demonstrate consistency across formats
- Include before/after comparisons where applicable

### Text Overlay Guidelines

**Font Selection:**
- Primary: Clean, modern sans-serif (Inter, Roboto, Open Sans)
- Secondary: Complementary font for emphasis
- Size: Readable at thumbnail size (24pt+)
- Color: High contrast against background

**Message Structure:**
- Headline: 3-5 words, maximum impact
- Subtext: Supporting benefit or metric
- Call-to-action: Clear next step
