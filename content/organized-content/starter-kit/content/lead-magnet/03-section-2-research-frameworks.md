# SECTION 2: RESEARCH FRAMEWORKS (Prompts 6-10)

---

## PROMPT 6: Plain English Explainer

**Purpose:** Understand complex regulations, policies, or laws in plain language.

**The Prompt:**
```
Explain [REGULATION/LAW/POLICY NAME] in plain English as if I have no legal or technical background.

Cover these elements:
- One-Sentence Summary: What it is in the simplest possible terms
- Who It Affects: Individuals, businesses, specific industries
- Key Requirements: What must be done to comply (bullet points)
- Common Exemptions: Who is excluded and why
- Practical Impact: How it affects an individual like me in [JURISDICTION]
- Official Source: Where to find the full text

Use analogies where helpful. Format as a 1-2 page executive summary.
```

**Pro Tip:** Run this prompt **twice**—once for a general explanation and once focused on your specific situation.

## PROMPT 7: Exemption Finder

**Purpose:** Identify possible exemptions or exceptions applicable to your situation.

**The Prompt:**
```
I need to understand possible exemptions for [PROCESS/REQUIREMENT] in [JURISDICTION].

My situation: [DESCRIBE YOUR CONTEXT IN 3-5 SENTENCES]

Research and list:
- Common exemptions or exceptions that might apply
- Criteria for qualification (what must be true)
- Application process (if required - steps, forms, fees)
- Relevant forms or agencies
- Official citations or links

Output as a clear checklist I can use to verify my eligibility.
```

**Pro Tip:** Exemptions often have **strict filing deadlines**. Add a "deadline check" to your research.

## PROMPT 8: Regulatory Comparison

**Purpose:** Compare two related regulations to understand which applies.

**The Prompt:**
```
Compare [REGULATION A] and [REGULATION B] for [CONTEXT].

Create a comparison table with these columns:
| Dimension | Regulation A | Regulation B |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| Scope/Applicability | | |
| Key Provisions | | |
| Compliance Requirements | | |
| Penalties for Non-Compliance | | |
| Exemptions | | |
| Which applies to a [INDIVIDUAL/SMALL BUSINESS] in [JURISDICTION] | | |

Add a summary section: "Which One Matters to You" with clear guidance.
```

**Pro Tip:** Use this prompt **before** drafting any formal communication. Knowing which framework applies prevents misstatements.

## PROMPT 9: Procedure Outline

**Purpose:** Get a step-by-step guide for a specific process.

**The Prompt:**
```
Outline the step-by-step procedure for [PROCESS: e.g., filing a complaint, appealing a decision, registering a document] in [JURISDICTION].

Assume I am an individual with no legal training.

Include:
- Prerequisites: What I need to have prepared before starting
- Step-by-Step Instructions: Numbered list with estimated time for each
- Forms Required: Names, numbers, and where to find them
- Fees: Amounts and payment methods
- Contact: Who to contact if stuck (agency/office with phone/email)
- What to Do If Stuck: Escalation path

Format: Printable checklist.
```

**Pro Tip:** Government websites often have **procedural guides** that aren't easy to find. Use this prompt on a government web page's text, not just your question.

## PROMPT 10: Rights & Obligations Summary

**Purpose:** Understand your rights and obligations in a specific context.

**The Prompt:**
```
Summarize the rights and obligations of an individual [IN CONTEXT: e.g., as a tenant, as a consumer, as a contractor] in [JURISDICTION].

Format as two clear lists:
- Rights I Have: [Numbered list with brief explanations]
- Obligations I Must Meet: [Numbered list with brief explanations]

Also include:
- Who Enforces These: Agency or body with authority
- Where to Get Help: Official sources only
- Common Pitfalls to Avoid: Mistakes people often make

Add a "Quick Reference" card at the end - a 1-page summary I can keep handy.
```

**Pro Tip:** Your rights are often **minimum standards**. What's listed is the floor, not the ceiling of what you can request.
