## Bot Integration FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: What does the Discord bot do?**
A: The Discord bot provides character prompts, generates AI art prompts, delivers character information, and assists with content creation directly within Discord servers. It supports slash commands, DM interactions, and server-wide announcements.

**Q: How do I invite the bot to my Discord server?**
A: Visit our website or Gumroad profile for the bot invite link. The Platform Integration Kit includes complete Discord bot setup with custom configuration for your server's needs.

**Q: Is the Discord bot free to use?**
A: Basic functionality is free with a 5 prompts per day limit per user. Premium subscription unlocks unlimited prompts, batch generation, and advanced features. Server-wide licenses are available for communities.

**Q: What commands does the Discord bot support?**
A: Supported slash commands include: `/prompt` for custom prompts, `/random` for random character prompts, `/character` for character information, `/batch` for multiple prompts (premium), `/subscribe` for subscription management, and `/help` for command reference.
