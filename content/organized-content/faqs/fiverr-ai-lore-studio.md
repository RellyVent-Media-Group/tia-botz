# Fiverr FAQ: AI Lore Studio & Narrative Design

**Q: What genres do you work with?**

> Science fiction, cyberpunk, fantasy, urban fantasy, horror, historical fiction, superhero, and branded/corporate narratives. If you're unsure, send me a message and I'll confirm.

**Q: I'm not a writer. Can you turn my ideas into a full world?**

> Absolutely. That's the core service. Send me a paragraph of your idea, 2-3 reference inspirations, and I'll build it out to the depth you need. The Basic tier is great for testing whether my style fits your vision.

**Q: Do you create maps or visual assets?**

> No, this service is text-only (lore, characters, descriptions, articles). I can describe layouts for visual reference, but I don't produce images. I recommend pairing my work with AI image generators or hiring an illustrator separately.

**Q: What's included in a "World Bible"?**

> 20+ pages covering: world origin/history, geography, faction descriptions with motivations, major NPCs/characters, magic/tech systems, timeline of key events, narrative hooks, and tone/style guidelines. It's designed to be handed directly to a creative team.

**Q: What are "Niche Content Packs"?**

> Themed bundles of SEO-friendly articles (500-800 words each) on any topic. For example: "10 Articles on Sustainable Living," "15 Articles on AI in Small Business," or "12 Articles on Digital Privacy." I research the niche, create a keyword matrix, then generate the pack.

**Q: Can I order both narrative design AND content packs together?**

> Yes. Select your tier and add the content pack as a gig extra. Or contact me directly and I'll create a custom quote.

**Q: What if I need more revisions on a complex world?**

> The Premium tier includes two revision rounds. For larger projects (whole campaigns), we can agree on a revision schedule upfront. I recommend smaller, iterative batches.

**Q: Will my lore be unique?**

> Yes. Every narrative is custom-built from your prompts and references. I do not reuse content across clients. All rights to the final work belong to you after delivery.

**Q: Can you write in a specific author's style?**

> Yes, with a caveat: I can approximate style (e.g., "like Neal Stephenson's tech density" or "like Ursula Le Guin's cultural focus") but I can't guarantee exact mimicry. Sharing 2-3 specific examples of prose you love helps greatly.

**Q: Do you offer voiceover narration scripts as well?**

> Yes, that's handled best through my **Gig A: AI Scriptwriter Pro**. For the Lore gig, I can include narration-style intros or ending monologues if requested.
